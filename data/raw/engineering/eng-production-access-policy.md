# Production Access SOP

## Purpose

This standard operating procedure describes how Engineering team
members obtain access to production systems and databases.

## Senior Engineer Access

**Senior Engineers and above are granted standing access to production
databases** as part of their role, without needing to file a
per-instance request, since production debugging is a core part of
the senior engineering function.

## Mid-Level and Junior Engineers

Mid-level and junior engineers must request temporary production
access through the on-call lead for the duration of a specific
incident or task.

## On-Call Rotation

The on-call engineer for each service has production access for the
duration of their rotation, coordinated through the on-call schedule
tool.

## Audit Logging

All production database queries are logged automatically regardless
of how access was granted.
