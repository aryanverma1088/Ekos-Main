# Expense Policy (Version 1.0)

## Purpose

This policy outlines how employees may submit and be reimbursed for
business-related expenses.

## Eligible Expenses

Eligible expenses include client meals, local travel, and approved
software subscriptions required for work.

## Reimbursement Limit

Employees may claim reimbursement for eligible expenses **up to $150
per month** without additional approval. Expenses above this amount
require sign-off from a department head.

## Submission Process

Expense claims must be submitted through the finance portal within 30
days of the expense being incurred, with a receipt attached.

## Travel Expenses

Travel expenses are covered separately under the Travel Expense
Policy.
