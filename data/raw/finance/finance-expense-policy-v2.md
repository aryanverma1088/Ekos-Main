# Expense Policy (Version 2.0)

## Purpose

This policy outlines how employees may submit and be reimbursed for
business-related expenses. This version supersedes Expense Policy
v1.0 in full.

## Eligible Expenses

Eligible expenses include client meals, local travel, approved
software subscriptions, and remote-work-related equipment up to the
limits described below.

## Reimbursement Limit

Employees may claim reimbursement for eligible expenses **up to $250
per month** without additional approval. This is an increase from the
$150 limit in the previous version, reflecting updated cost-of-living
guidance from the Finance department. Expenses above this amount
require sign-off from a department head.

## Submission Process

Expense claims must be submitted through the finance portal within 30
days of the expense being incurred, with a receipt attached.

## Travel Expenses

Travel expenses are covered separately under the Travel Expense
Policy.

## Review

This policy is reviewed annually alongside the Procurement Policy.
