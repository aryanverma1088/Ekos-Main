# Procurement Policy

## Purpose

This policy governs how departments at Aurelia Technologies purchase
goods and services from external vendors.

## Purchase Approval Thresholds

Purchases under $1,000 may be approved directly by a team lead.
Purchases between $1,000 and $10,000 require department head approval.
Purchases above $10,000 require Finance and executive sign-off.

## Preferred Vendors

Departments are encouraged to use the preferred vendor list maintained
by Finance, which offers negotiated corporate rates.

## New Vendor Onboarding

New vendors must pass a basic compliance and security review before
any contract is signed, coordinated between Finance and IT Security.

## Software Purchases

All new software subscriptions must be logged in the software asset
register, regardless of purchase amount, to support license
compliance audits.
