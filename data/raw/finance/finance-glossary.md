# Finance Glossary of Terms

## Purpose

This glossary defines key financial and accounting terms as used
consistently across Finance department reporting and forecasting.

## Customer

For financial reporting purposes, a **Customer is defined as a paying
account** — an organization or individual that has an active, billed
subscription or contract with Aurelia Technologies. Prospective or
trial accounts are not counted as customers in revenue reporting.

## Revenue Recognition

Revenue is recognized monthly, in line with the subscription term of
each customer contract, following standard accrual accounting
practices.

## Churned Account

A churned account is a former customer whose contract has ended and
has not been renewed within 30 days of expiration.

## Annual Recurring Revenue (ARR)

ARR is calculated as the total contracted recurring revenue,
normalized to a 12-month period, across all active customer accounts.
