# Parental Leave Policy

## Purpose

This policy describes parental leave entitlements for employees of
Aurelia Technologies following the birth or adoption of a child.

## Eligibility

All full-time employees who have completed six months of continuous
employment are eligible for parental leave under this policy.

## Entitlement

Eligible employees are entitled to **18 weeks of paid parental leave**,
which may be taken continuously or split into two blocks within the
first 12 months following the birth or adoption of the child.

## Notice Period

Employees should notify their manager and HR at least 8 weeks before
the expected start of parental leave, where practicable.

## Pay During Leave

Parental leave is paid at 100% of the employee's base salary for the
full duration of the leave.

## Return to Work

Employees returning from parental leave are guaranteed the right to
return to their previous role or an equivalent role at the same level.
