# Remote Work Policy (Version 3.0)

## Purpose

This policy defines the conditions under which employees of Aurelia
Technologies may work remotely instead of attending the office in person.

## Eligibility

All full-time employees who have completed their 90-day probationary
period are eligible to request remote work arrangements, subject to
manager approval.

## Remote Work Allowance

Employees may work remotely up to **2 days per week**. Remote days must
be agreed with the employee's direct manager in advance and logged in
the internal HR system.

## Core Hours

Employees working remotely are expected to be reachable between 10:00
and 16:00 local time on their assigned remote days.

## Equipment

Aurelia Technologies will provide a company laptop for remote work.
Employees are responsible for maintaining a secure home network
connection in line with the IT Security Policy.

## Review

This policy is reviewed annually by HR in coordination with department
heads.
