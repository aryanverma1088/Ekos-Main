# Leave Policy

## Purpose

This policy explains how employees should request planned time away
from work and what advance notice is required.

## Annual Leave

Full-time employees accrue 22 days of annual leave per calendar year,
prorated for employees who join partway through the year.

## Requesting Leave

Employees must submit leave requests at least 48 hours before the
planned absence, using the HR portal. Requests submitted with less
notice may be declined at the manager's discretion, except in cases
of emergency.

## Approval

Leave requests are approved by the employee's direct manager. Approved
leave is automatically reflected in the shared team calendar.

## Unused Leave

Up to 5 days of unused annual leave may be carried over into the
following calendar year.
