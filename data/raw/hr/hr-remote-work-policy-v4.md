# Remote Work Policy (Version 4.0)

## Purpose

This policy defines the conditions under which employees of Aurelia
Technologies may work remotely instead of attending the office in person.
This version supersedes Remote Work Policy v3.0 in full.

## Eligibility

All full-time employees who have completed their 90-day probationary
period are eligible to request remote work arrangements, subject to
manager approval.

## Remote Work Allowance

Employees may work remotely **up to 3 days per week**. Remote days must
be agreed with the employee's direct manager in advance and logged in
the internal HR system. This is an increase from the 2-day allowance
in the previous version, reflecting employee feedback collected in the
2024 engagement survey.

## Core Hours

Employees working remotely are expected to be reachable between 10:00
and 16:00 local time on their assigned remote days.

## Equipment and Security

Aurelia Technologies will provide a company laptop for remote work.
Employees must comply with the current IT Security Policy and Device
Policy, including full-disk encryption and use of the company VPN when
accessing internal systems.

## Related Policies

This policy should be read alongside the Attendance Policy, Office
Access Policy, and IT Security Policy.

## Review

This policy is reviewed annually by HR in coordination with department
heads. Next scheduled review: Q4 2025.
