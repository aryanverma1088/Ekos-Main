# Employee Handbook

## Welcome to Aurelia Technologies

This handbook provides a general overview of company culture, benefits,
and everyday policies for all employees.

## Working Hours

Standard working hours are 9:00 to 18:00, Monday through Friday, with
a one-hour lunch break.

## Time Off

Leave applications should be submitted two days before the planned
absence wherever possible. Employees should use the HR portal to
submit requests and check their remaining leave balance.

## Workplace Conduct

Employees are expected to treat colleagues with respect and follow the
Code of Conduct outlined in Section 7 of this handbook.

## Benefits Overview

Aurelia Technologies offers health insurance, a retirement savings
plan, and an annual wellness stipend. Full details are available on
the internal benefits portal.

## Questions

For questions not covered in this handbook, employees should consult
the relevant department-specific policy or contact HR directly.
