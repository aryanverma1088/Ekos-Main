# Performance Review Policy

## Purpose

This policy describes how employee performance is evaluated at Aurelia
Technologies, and how performance reviews connect to compensation and
promotion decisions.

## Review Cycle

Formal performance reviews are conducted twice per year, in June and
December. Each review covers the preceding six-month period and
includes a self-assessment, manager assessment, and for employees at
senior levels, peer feedback gathered through the internal feedback
tool.

## Rating Scale

Performance is rated on a five-point scale ranging from "significantly
below expectations" to "significantly exceeds expectations." Ratings
are calibrated across teams within each department to ensure
consistency, coordinated by HR and department heads before ratings are
finalized and shared with employees.

## Goal Setting

At the start of each review cycle, employees and managers agree on 3 to
5 goals for the upcoming period. Goals should be specific and
measurable where possible. Progress against goals from the previous
cycle is a primary input into the performance rating, though it is not
the only factor considered.

## Compensation Connection

Annual compensation reviews occur in December, informed by but not
mechanically determined by the December performance rating. Department
heads have discretion within budget guidelines set by Finance and HR
each year.

## Promotions

Promotion decisions are considered during the December review cycle for
most roles. Employees being considered for promotion typically need a
sustained pattern of "exceeds expectations" or higher ratings over the
prior year, along with manager and, for senior roles, a promotion
committee's endorsement.

## Performance Improvement Plans

Employees rated "below expectations" or "significantly below
expectations" are placed on a performance improvement plan (PIP) in
partnership with HR, with clear expectations and a defined timeline,
typically 60 to 90 days, for demonstrating improvement.

## Confidentiality

Performance ratings and review content are confidential and shared only
with the employee, their manager, and HR, except where required for
calibration or promotion committee review.
