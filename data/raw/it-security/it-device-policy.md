# Device Policy

## Scope

This policy applies to all company-issued laptops, phones, and tablets
used by Aurelia Technologies employees.

## Company-Issued Laptops

All laptops must run the standard corporate image and receive
security patches automatically through the managed update channel.

## Encryption Requirement

Full disk encryption must be enabled on every company laptop before
the device is handed over to an employee. This is a mandatory step in
the IT provisioning checklist and cannot be skipped.

## Personal Devices (BYOD)

Personal devices used to access company email must have a passcode
enabled and must be enrolled in the mobile device management (MDM)
system.

## Lost or Stolen Devices

Lost or stolen devices must be reported to IT Security immediately so
that remote wipe procedures can be initiated.

## Device Return

All company devices must be returned to IT upon termination of
employment or role change involving equipment reassignment.
