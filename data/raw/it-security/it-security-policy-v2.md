# IT Security Policy

## Purpose

This policy establishes minimum security requirements for all systems
and accounts used at Aurelia Technologies.

## Password Requirements

Passwords must be at least 12 characters long and include a mix of
upper-case letters, lower-case letters, numbers, and symbols.
**Passwords must be changed every 90 days.** Reuse of the previous 5
passwords is not permitted.

## Multi-Factor Authentication

All employees must enable multi-factor authentication (MFA) on their
company accounts, including email, VPN, and source control systems.

## Device Encryption

All company-issued laptops must have **full-disk encryption enabled**
before they are used to access internal systems. IT will verify
encryption status during the device provisioning process.

## Incident Reporting

Suspected security incidents must be reported to the IT Security team
within 1 hour of discovery via the internal incident channel.

## Enforcement

Non-compliance with this policy may result in restricted account
access until the issue is resolved.
