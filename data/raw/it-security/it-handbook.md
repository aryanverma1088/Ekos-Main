# IT Handbook

## Getting Started

This handbook is a practical guide for employees setting up and using
their IT accounts and equipment at Aurelia Technologies.

## Account Setup

New employees receive their account credentials from IT on their first
day. Initial passwords must be changed on first login.

## Password Guidance

For convenience, most employees find it easier to update their
password roughly twice a year. **Passwords must be changed every 180
days** under standard account settings, though employees are welcome
to change them more frequently.

## VPN Access

The company VPN client can be downloaded from the internal software
portal. Contact the IT helpdesk if you experience connection issues.

## Requesting Equipment

New equipment requests should be submitted through the IT ticketing
system with manager approval attached.

## Support

The IT helpdesk is available Monday–Friday, 8:00–18:00, via the
internal chat channel #it-support.
