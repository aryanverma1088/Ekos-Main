# Access Control Policy

## Purpose

This policy defines who may access sensitive internal systems,
including production databases and infrastructure.

## Principle of Least Privilege

Access to any system must be granted on a need-to-know basis and
reviewed quarterly by IT Security.

## Production Database Access

Access to production databases requires **explicit manager approval
and a completed access request ticket**, regardless of seniority or
role. All production database access also requires multi-factor
authentication and is logged for audit purposes.

## Access Reviews

IT Security conducts quarterly access reviews and revokes any
permissions that are no longer required for an employee's current
role.

## Emergency Access

In a declared incident, on-call engineers may be granted temporary
emergency access, which is automatically revoked after 24 hours and
logged for post-incident review.

## Policy Precedence

Where this policy conflicts with department-specific procedures, this
Access Control Policy takes precedence for anything involving
production systems.
