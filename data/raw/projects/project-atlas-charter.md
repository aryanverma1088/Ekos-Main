# Project Atlas Charter

## Overview

Project Atlas is Aurelia Technologies' initiative to improve
top-of-funnel lead generation and conversion tracking, led by the
Marketing team in collaboration with Sales.

## Goals

- Increase qualified lead volume by 25% within two quarters
- Improve lead-to-signup conversion tracking across channels
- Establish a shared funnel dashboard for Marketing and Sales

## Definitions

For the purposes of this project and Marketing/Sales funnel reporting,
a **Customer is defined as any qualified lead** that has entered the
sales funnel and been marked "sales-qualified" by the SDR team,
regardless of whether they have yet converted to a paid account. This
broader definition is used to measure funnel health and pipeline
velocity.

## Stakeholders

- Marketing (project owner)
- Sales
- Product (for onboarding flow input)

## Timeline

Project Atlas runs for two quarters starting Q1 2024, with a
mid-project review in Q2.
